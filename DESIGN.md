# Design System: The Times

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Halftone Broadsheet"**

A real newspaper whose printing technique is the idea: everything printed is dots. The globe, the charts, the meters and the photographs all come off the same press, so a reader never crosses from "the paper" into "the dashboard". Numbers are written as sentences first and shown as tables second. Density is a broadsheet's: multiple text columns, hairline column rules, double rules between sections, and a lot on the page at once.

The paper runs on four stocks, set as token sets on `[data-theme]`. Morning is financial-paper pink with black ink and claret. Night is the same page as a negative plate with rose. Cobalt is a drenched blue where one colour owns the page and no serif appears. Glass is frosted panes over slow daylight. A theme changes stock, ink, type, radius and heavy-rule weight, and never the content or the structure of the page. Morning is the default by day and Night after dark until the reader picks a plate.

The motion is ink and paper: the nameplate settles, columns rise in reading order, a circular plate-change wipe swaps stock, a detail page turns up over the old sheet, and desks rise once on scroll. It rejects the dashboard of tiles and the cream-and-serif editorial page.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One visual idea, halftone, carried by the globe, meters, odds, posters, photographs and charts.
- Four complete themes built from one token shape, with the same page structure in each.
- Rules, not boxes, organise the page in Morning, Night and Cobalt; floating panes do it in Glass.
- Spot colour is rationed, and status colour never carries meaning alone.
- Self-hosted type only, with a display, a text and a data voice.

## Colors

Each theme is a stock, an ink family in three strengths, one spot colour, a set of status inks and three globe plates. Tokens are prefixed by theme in the frontmatter (`morning-`, `night-`, `cobalt-`, `glass-`) and keep the same role across all four; the CSS custom property for each is in the sidecar.

### Primary
- **Spot** (`*-spot`): Claret in Morning, rose in Night, signal yellow in Cobalt, violet in Glass. Link hover, focus ring, the drop cap, the reading-progress rule, list bullets, the locator ring, and the "on" dots of the sales chart.

### Secondary
- **Globe plates** (`*-plate-conflict`, `*-plate-economy`, `*-plate-hazard`): Three spot inks for the globe's data layers and their chips. The same three inks are the series colours in a multi-series dot plot, in that order.

### Tertiary
- **Status inks** (`*-good`, `*-warn`, `*-bad`): Health. Reserved for systems state, stale data and destructive feedback.
- **Market inks** (`*-pos`, `*-neg`): Direction of money and markets. These always sit beside a triangle or a sign. The tape drops them entirely and prints in Secondary Ink, so the triangle carries the direction.

### Neutral
- **Stock** (`*-stock`): The page. Also the text colour on inverted ink blocks (tooltip, banner, 4K badge, selected segment).
- **Tint** (`*-tint`): The second stock, behind images while they load and in a focused cost field. In Glass it is translucent white.
- **Ink** (`*-ink`), **Secondary Ink** (`*-ink-2`), **Tertiary Ink** (`*-ink-3`): Text at three strengths. Secondary for decks, datelines and sublabels; Tertiary for timestamps, source lines and empty values. Morning's Tertiary Ink is 4.4:1 on the stock and 4.0:1 on Tint, short of 4.5:1; it is recorded because it shipped, and it should be darkened, not matched.
- **Rule** (`*-rule`): Strong rules. Equal to Ink, except in Glass where it is a 15% tint.
- **Hairline** (`*-hair`): Row and column rules, the unfilled dots of a meter, the off dots of a chart, and quiet hover fills.
- **Globe Ink** (`*-globe-ink`): The land plate.
- **Selection** (`*-select`, `*-select-ink`): Text selection.

### Named Rules
**The Same Shape Rule.** Every theme fills the same token set. A new colour role goes into all four themes or it does not ship.

**The Icon And Label Rule.** Status colour always ships with a shape and words: the filled circle for good, the triangle for warn, the crossed strokes for bad, the open ring for a note, each beside its text. Hue alone cannot carry it, because Night's bad equals its spot and Cobalt's warn equals its spot. Four shipped spots fall short (the uptime dots, meter warn and bad, the Systems glance value, the stale source line); those are defects, not precedent.

**The Ration Rule.** Spot colour marks interaction and a few set pieces. It never fills a section, a headline or a table row.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Frank Ruhl Libre (with Times New Roman, serif) in Morning and Night; Archivo at its widest in Cobalt; Schibsted Grotesk in Glass
**Body Font:** Source Serif 4 (with Georgia, serif) in Morning and Night; Schibsted Grotesk in Cobalt and Glass
**Label/Mono Font:** Schibsted Grotesk (with system-ui, sans-serif) in every theme, for data, labels and controls

**Character:** A heavy newspaper serif over a sturdy text serif, with a neutral grotesque handling every number. Cobalt swaps the page for expanded uppercase grotesque; Glass softens it to one sans.

Headline weight and tracking are theme tokens (`--head-weight`, `--head-track`): 800 and -.015em in Morning and Night, 900 and -.03em in Cobalt, 700 and -.025em in Glass. All four families are variable fonts served from `/fonts`, with Frank Ruhl Libre, Source Serif 4 and Schibsted Grotesk preloaded.

### Hierarchy
- **Nameplate** (head weight, clamp to 104px, .98): The masthead only. Centred. Cobalt sets it left, uppercase, at 125% width, up to 112px. Glass reduces it to 58px.
- **Headline** (head weight, clamp to 62px, 1.02): The front-page lead, balanced.
- **Page Head** (head weight, clamp to 66px, 1.03): The title of a detail page.
- **Desk Name** (head weight, clamp to 52px, 1): Section titles over a solid rule. Cobalt blows these up to 120px, uppercase and expanded, with a 4px rule.
- **Sub Head** (head weight, clamp to 38px, 1.08): Lead item within a desk.
- **Title** (800, 21px, 1.14): Story and rail headings. Smaller display cuts run 15px to 19px for wire, poster and agenda titles.
- **Deck** (italic 400, clamp to 22px, 1.34): The standfirst, in Secondary Ink. Upright 500 in Cobalt and Glass.
- **Body** (400, 17px, 1.55): Default text. The lead runs 16.5px in two columns, and stories 16px.
- **Reading** (400, 19px, 1.58; 18px on phones): Article bodies, capped at 66ch.
- **Data** (400 to 500, 15px, 1.3): Tables, facts and lists, in lining numerals.
- **Label** (700, 15px, 1.2): Table captions, group headings. Controls run 600 at 13px to 14px, line height 1.
- **Meta** (400, 13px, 1.3): Bylines, source lines, timestamps. The floor is 11px, for the 4K badge and citation pills.
- **Drop Cap** (800, 3.5em, .82): The lead's first letter, in Spot. Morning and Night only.

### Named Rules
**The Three Voices Rule.** Headlines speak in the display face, reading in the body face, and numbers and controls in the data face. A figure inside a table is never set in a serif.

**The Upright Rule.** Italic belongs to Source Serif 4. Cobalt and Glass ship no italic face, so emphasis there is weight.

**The No Kicker Rule.** A headline stands on its own. Date and edition live in the masthead ears, and a story's source lives under it.

## Layout

The page is a rule-divided broadsheet held inside a fluid gutter (16px to 40px at 3vw). The front is three columns at 2.4 : 5.2 : 4.6 (note, lead, globe), followed by a top-stories band at 2 : 1 : 1 : 1 and the five desks in order. A sticky section bar sits under the masthead at 48px minimum, holding the desks, the markets tape and four theme plates, and anchors land 72px below the top.

Long text uses CSS multi-column with a hairline column rule: two columns in the lead, up to three in the brief and agenda, two in the wire. Stories and days never break across columns. Prose is capped at 66ch to 74ch. Detail pages are one reading column plus a 220px to 280px rail of facts, within 1180px (1240px in Glass).

Spacing is a tight print rhythm of 6, 8, 10, 14, 18 and 22px inside blocks. List rows are padded 7px to 11px and separated by hairlines rather than gaps. Between columns it is 32px in text flows and fluid (20px to 40px) in grids.

At 1180px the front drops to two columns with the note spanning the top, and top stories go two-up. At 860px everything is one column, the section bar wraps with desks on their own row, Inside is hidden, top stories become 112px thumbnail rows, and the machine table folds into three labelled cells per host. Phone order is nameplate, bar, note, glance, lead, globe.

### Named Rules
**The Phone Target Rule.** At 860px and below every control is at least 44px tall (`.seg`, layer chips, inputs, the locate button, theaters and service rows all step up to meet it). Desktop controls may run 36px to 40px. The Reload button, `.linkish` text buttons and table sort headers ship below this on phones; those are defects, not precedent.

**The Same Sentence Rule.** Numbers appear as a sentence or a glance row first and a table second. The tape is a summary of a table, never the only place a figure lives.

## Elevation & Depth

Morning, Night and Cobalt are flat: depth is ink on paper, and hierarchy comes from rule weight. Only the transient globe pick and the tooltip lift, with a soft, low-opacity shadow. Glass is the deliberate exception, a layered world where panes float over a slowly drifting blurred light field (`.ambient`, four radial washes under 70px blur) with `backdrop-filter`, a white inner top edge and a soft blue-black shadow. The ambient field exists only in Glass, because it gives the panes something to frost.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 24px #0004`): The tooltip. The globe pick uses the same shadow at `0 10px 30px #0004`.
- **Glass Sheet** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 50px #1b23401c, inset 0 1px 0 #ffffffe6`): A desk pane, with `backdrop-filter: blur(28px) saturate(165%)` and a 1px white border.
- **Glass Bar** (`box-shadow: 0 10px 34px #1b234024, inset 0 1px 0 #ffffffd9`): The floating pill section bar, 10px from the top, blur 22px.
- **Plate Ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 2px stock, 0 0 0 4px ink`): The selected theme plate.

### Named Rules
**The Print Is Flat Rule.** In the three printed themes a shadow means the thing is not on the paper. Anything that prints stays flat.

## Shapes

Print rectangles are square in Morning, Night and Cobalt (`--radius: 0`) and the dot is the native round form everywhere. Glass raises the radius to 24px for panes and the rest follows by cap, so each element sets `min(var(--radius), cap)`: 5px for the 4K badge and agenda thumbnails, 6px for globe labels, 10px for inputs and icon buttons, 12px for notes and banners, 14px for posters and photos, 18px for hero images. Layer chips, citation pills and meter dots are round in every theme.

Rules are the main form. There are three weights: a heavy double rule (`--heavy` double) between sections, a solid 1px rule under section titles, and a hairline between rows and columns. `--heavy` is 3px in Morning and Night, 4px in Cobalt and 1px in Glass, where sections separate by space and panes instead. Posters are 2:3, the globe is a circle in a square, and hero backdrops are 21:8 (16:9 on phones).

### Named Rules
**The Capped Radius Rule.** Never hard-code a corner on a print rectangle. Use `min(var(--radius), cap)` so it stays square in three themes and softens in Glass.

**The Three Rules Rule.** Separation is one of three rule weights, chosen by the size of the break. No fourth weight.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet, typographic and square, more printed mark than widget.
- **Shape:** Square in print themes, capped at 10px in Glass (12px for the segmented group).
- **Segmented (`.seg`):** 1px Rule border, 600 13.5px, 40px tall (44px on phones). Selected inverts to Ink with Stock text.
- **Ghost text:** 1px Hairline border, 600 14px, 44px tall. Hover strengthens the border to Ink.
- **Ghost icon:** 44px box with a 16px stroked SVG in Secondary Ink. A delete hover turns Bad over a Hairline fill.
- **Focus:** 2px Spot outline at 2px offset, everywhere.

### Chips
- **Layer chip:** A pill, 1px Hairline border, 600 13px, with a 9px dot in the plate colour. 36px tall, 44px on phones. Off state is 50% opacity with a line through it, so it never relies on colour.
- **Citation pill (`.ref`):** Hairline fill, 700 11px, inverts on hover.

### Cards / Containers
There are no stat cards. A desk is a section under a rule; in Glass the same `.sheet` is a pane at 46% white, radius 24px, padded 18px to 34px, carrying a whole desk rather than a number.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** Inline edit. Transparent with a transparent border and no box at rest, 500 15px, 40px tall (44px and 16px type on phones), so a cost list reads as a printed ledger.
- **Hover / Focus:** Hover shows a Hairline border. Focus shows an Ink border on Tint.
- **Error:** `aria-invalid` shows a Bad border. A blank cost stays blank.

### Navigation
- **Section bar:** Sticky, 600 14px, heavy rule above and hairline below. Desk links are 46px tall; the current desk carries a 2px Ink underline. A 2px Spot rule along the bottom tracks reading progress on a scroll timeline.
- **Markets tape:** A 70s linear marquee of labels, triangles and percentages, faded at both edges. Pauses on hover, and on tap for touch.
- **Theme plates:** Four 26px discs in 44px buttons, each a tiny print of its stock: dot-screened pink, dot-screened black, flat cobalt, conic glass. A radio group, with hover scale 1.14 and the Plate Ring when checked.
- **Glass:** The bar becomes a floating pill.

### Halftone Globe
The signature. A WebGL2 fragment shader prints each layer as its own plate on its own screen angle, with tone carried by dot area: land 45 degrees in Globe Ink, conflict 75, economy 15, hazards 135. Cell size is `max(3.2px, R / 62)` in device pixels, and the tone is read at each dot's centre so dots never melt into blobs. Conflict and hazard dots breathe gently. It idles at 5.5 degrees a second, drags, keeps inertia, responds to arrow keys, filters by layer chip, and flies to a theater from a locate button. Colours come from the theme tokens and re-ink after a plate change. Without WebGL the stage and chips are removed and the theater list stands alone. Detail pages reuse it as a small still locator with a 22px Spot ring.

### Halftone Meter
Two radial-gradient dot rows on a 7.4 by 9px grid: Hairline dots for the track and Ink dots clipped to `--v` percent for the value. Warn and Bad swap the ink at 80 and 92 percent, and width animates over .9s. Odds use the same mark.

### Halftone Image
Posters, photographs, backdrops and heroes share one treatment. `--poster-filter` turns the image greyscale, `--poster-blend` lays it into the stock (multiply in Morning, screen in Night and Cobalt), and `--poster-screen` adds a 2.6px dot screen tinted to the stock. Hover or focus reveals full colour in .6s with a slight scale; a hero toggles on tap. Glass sets all three to none, so images print in full colour.

### Dot Charts
The one chart language. `dotPlot` in `web/lib.js` draws every time series as dots on a fitted scale with three hairline gridlines and plain-number labels, and hover or tap reads a point. Sales per day is a stacked column of dots, and service uptime is one dot an hour for seven days. Unmeasured hours print as a half-size Hairline dot, never as a guess.

### Status Line
A 12px shape icon from `ICON` in `web/lib.js`, then the words. Used for hosts, services, worries, disks, pools, backups and failed tasks.

### Masthead Ears
Date on the left and edition on the right, 500 13px in Secondary Ink, flanking the nameplate.

### Motion
All motion uses `--ease` (`cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)`) except linear scroll-linked runs; durations are in the sidecar. Under `prefers-reduced-motion`, CSS animation and transition are off, smooth scrolling is off, the tape loses its mask and becomes a manual horizontal scroll, the plate wipe and page turn become instant swaps, and the globe stops its idle spin and dot breathing. Dragging and flying the globe still move, because the reader asked for them. On phones the Glass ambient drift is off.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** derive any new visual from dots first: a new measure is a meter, an odds row or a `dotPlot`.
- **Do** define every new colour role in all four `[data-theme]` blocks, and test it in all four.
- **Do** pair every status colour with its shape icon and a word.
- **Do** pass images through `--poster-filter`, `--poster-blend` and `--poster-screen`, with the colour reveal on hover or focus.
- **Do** use `min(var(--radius), cap)` on rectangles and `var(--heavy) double var(--line)` between sections.
- **Do** give every control a 44px height at 860px and below, with a 2px Spot focus outline.
- **Do** read `prefers-reduced-motion` in JS for any canvas or scripted motion, as well as relying on the CSS kill switch.
- **Do** keep printed copy free of em dashes, and write numbers as a sentence before a table.
- **Do** self-host type and draw icons as inline SVG.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add a bar, line or pie chart, or a second chart language.
- **Don't** build dashboard tiles of big numbers in boxes.
- **Don't** drift toward the cream-and-serif editorial page; the stock is pink, black, cobalt or glass.
- **Don't** let a theme change content, order or structure; only stock, ink, type, radius and rule weight.
- **Don't** use a shadow on anything that prints in Morning, Night or Cobalt.
- **Don't** use frosted panes, `backdrop-filter` or the ambient light field outside Glass.
- **Don't** set italic in Cobalt or Glass.
- **Don't** use `--good`, `--warn` or `--bad` for decoration.
- **Don't** put a kicker or eyebrow over a headline.
